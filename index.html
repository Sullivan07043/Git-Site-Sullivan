Selected Research

EMNLP 2026 Under Review LLM Safety

SCOUT: A Predictor-Guided System for Prompt Injection Detection via Detector Fingerprints

  • Designed a pre-hoc risk routing framework inspired by cognitive accounts of expert intuition, modeling fast pre-hoc judgment through historical experience and short calibrated reasoning.
  • Built a lightweight risk estimator that routes untrusted inputs to defense tiers of increasing strength, from heuristic filtering to stronger protective mechanisms.
  • Investigated the safety–cost trade-off of adaptive defense policies, achieving lower attack success rates with minimal degradation on benign tasks.
ICML 2026 Accepted LLM Routing · RL

Models Under SCOPE: Scalable and Controllable Routing via Pre-hoc Reasoning

  • Developed a budget-aware LLM routing framework that predicts per-model answer correctness and token cost from retrieved anchor evidence, enabling controllable cost–quality trade-offs at inference time.
  • Built an anchor-based model fingerprint pipeline to support newly added / unseen models without per-task re-tuning.
  • Trained the routing policy with reinforcement learning to optimize utility under deployment constraints — up to 95.1% cost reduction or 25.7% accuracy gain under different priorities.

Qi Cao*, Shuhao Zhang*, Ruizhe Zhou, Ruiyi Zhang, Peijia Qin, Pengtao Xie. ICML 2026. arXiv:2601.22323. Project page ↗
