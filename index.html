About

I'm Shuhao Zhang, a Graduate Student Researcher and M.S. student in Computer Science and Engineering at UC San Diego, advised by Prof. Pengtao Xie. I also work with Prof. Yujia Zheng at UIUC. Before that I studied Big Data Management and Application at the University of Science and Technology Beijing.

My research studies how an agent processes its environment, experience, and memory into abstract understanding. This understanding is what gives an agent a real grasp of the world it acts in, and what lets it keep improving the decisions and actions it takes. Carried far enough, the same ability should let a system understand its own architecture and behavior, and improve those as well.

I'm currently looking for PhD positions starting Fall 2027.

ICLR 2027 · Under Review

How Causality Bridges the Semantic Gap

Shuhao Zhang, Xuran Zhou, Han Guo, Pengtao Xie, Yujia Zheng

Measurements record how a system behaves, yet they seldom say what its variables mean. CausalBridge reads the meaning of each unnamed variable from the causal role that it plays, and names observed and latent variables in questionnaires and robot systems more accurately than methods that rely on association.

EMNLP 2026 · Main

Send a SCOUT First: Pre-hoc Reasoning for Adaptive Detector Allocation in Prompt-Injection Defense

Shuhao Zhang†, Jiarui Li†, Qi Cao, Ruiyi Zhang, Pengtao Xie

Every prompt-injection detector stops some attacks and misses others. SCOUT predicts each detector's reliability and latency from how it behaved on similar inputs, then decides per request which detectors to run and whether to call an LLM judge.

Against an always-on GPT-4o judge: 46% lower attack success and 40% less wall-clock time, at a 5.1-point benign-utility cost.

ICML 2026

Models Under SCOPE: Scalable and Controllable Routing via Pre-hoc Reasoning

Qi Cao†, Shuhao Zhang†, Ruizhe Zhou, Ruiyi Zhang, Peijia Qin, Pengtao Xie

A service with many LLMs must choose one model for each query. SCOPE predicts the accuracy and the cost of every candidate model before any of them runs, so the router can trade cost for accuracy and handle models it has never seen.

  • up to 95.1% lower cost
  • up to 25.7% higher accuracy

Preprint 2026

LLMs Know When They Know, but Do Not Act on It: A Metacognitive Harness for Test-time Scaling

Qi Cao, Yufan Wang, Peijia Qin, Shuhao Zhang, Pengtao Xie

Strong LLMs can tell whether they know an answer, yet they do not act on it. The harness asks a frozen model for a feeling of knowing before it solves a problem and a judgment of learning after, and uses the two signals to accept, retry, or aggregate.
